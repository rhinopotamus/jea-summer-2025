Exercise 9.

Um Archivo data consultant group reported earnings of $42.7 billion in 2012.

Aside
(Story also appears in 7)
🔗
At that time executives projected 17% increase in earnings annually. Based on that information, we wrote the equation
\begin{equation*} U = 42.7 \ast 1.17^Y \end{equation*}
where \(U\) is Um Archivo’s reported earnings (in $billions) and \(Y\) is the years since 2012.
