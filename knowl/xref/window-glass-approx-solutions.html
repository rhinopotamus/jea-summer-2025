Exercise 7.

Suppose a special kind of window glass is 1 inch thick and lets through only 75% of the light.

Aside
(Story also appears in 7)
🔗
If two panes of this glass are used, the product is 2 inches thick and lets in 56.25% since
\begin{equation*} 75\% \text{ of } 75\% = (0.75)(0.75) = 0.5625 = 56.25\% \end{equation*}
