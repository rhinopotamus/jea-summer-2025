An air temperature of 10°F is cold but manageable. But add a 30 miles per hour wind and, brrr, it feels like it is -12°F (12 below zero). We say the wind chill of 10°F with a 30 mph wind is -12°F. The table lists the wind chill for various wind speeds at an air temperature of 10°F.
Wind speed (mph) 0 5 10 15 20 25 30 35 40 45 50 55
Wind chill (°F) 10 1 -4 -7 -9 -11 -12 -14 -15 -16 -17 -18
