Exercise 9.

After his first beer, Stephen’s blood alcohol content (BAC) was already 0.04 and as he continued to drink, his BAC level rose 45% per hour.

Aside
(Story also appears in 4.b and 1)
🔗
The equation is
\begin{equation*} S = 0.04 \ast 1.45^H \end{equation*}
where \(S\) is Stephen’s BAC and \(H\) is the time, measured in hours.
